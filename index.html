02 / About

A human-centred lens for digital products and visual communication.

Hello, I’m Caitlyn, a fourth-year Global Business and Digital Arts student at the University of Waterloo. I’ve always been curious about how people interact with the world around them, which is what drew me to the world of business, design, and technology.

I also love being involved in community and creating experiences that bring people together, whether that’s helping organize an event, promoting a greater cause, or simply finding ways to make people feel welcome and connected.

Outside of school and work, I love travelling, badminton, volleyball, and trying new things. You’ll usually find me playing Teamfight Tactics, trying a recipe I saw on social media, or spending time with my two cats.
